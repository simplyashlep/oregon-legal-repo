To Whom It May Concern:

This is a direct dispute under FCRA \S~1681s-2(a)(8) of Account *3419 and a notice of Oregon UCC Article 9 (ORS Chapter 79) violations.

\subsection*{1. Failure of Mandatory Pre-Sale and Post-Sale Notice (ORS 79.0611, 79.0614, 79.0616)}
For a consumer-goods transaction, a secured party must send a reasonable authenticated notification of disposition before sale (ORS 79.0611/79.0614) and a written explanation of any deficiency after sale (ORS 79.0616). Steel Lending Group sold the vehicle without sending the required Notice of Intent to Sell and did not send the post-sale deficiency explanation. A self-generated computer form with no proof of postage or certified-mail receipt is not proof of service.

\subsection*{2. Deficiency Barred; Statutory Damages (ORS 79.0626, 79.0625)}
Because compliance is placed in issue, Steel Lending Group bears the burden of proving compliance (ORS 79.0626). Absent proof of the required notices, it is barred from collecting any deficiency, and the \$13,635 balance is uncollectible and void --- so reporting it violates FCRA \S~1681e(b). In a consumer-goods transaction, ORS 79.0625(3)(b) also entitles the Consumer to statutory damages (the credit-service charge plus ten percent of the principal).
