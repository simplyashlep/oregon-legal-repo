Introduction
Aptitude is a legal intelligence system designed to transform unstructured human
legal experiences into structured, queryable legal knowledge.
The system operates as a multi-layer architecture consisting of:
• A structured incident intake system
• A legal knowledge graph
• A vector-based semantic retrieval layer
• A procedural reasoning engine
• A response composition layer

Legal Ontology System (Full Specification)
The Aptitude system is grounded in a formal ontology that defines all legal events,
claims, doctrines, remedies, and procedural steps as structured, queryable entities.
This ontology enables deterministic mapping between lived experiences and legal
reasoning pathways.
8.1
Top-Level Ontology Classes
The system defines six primary entity classes:
• Event: Real-world occurrence reported by a user
• Claim: Legally cognizable theory of harm
• Doctrine: Interpretive legal principle
• Statute: Codified legal rule (ORS, USC, etc.)
• Policy: Agency or institutional operational rule
• Remedy: Available legal or administrative action

• Agents are stateless and replaceable

• LLM usage is strictly constrained to classification and retrieval assistance
