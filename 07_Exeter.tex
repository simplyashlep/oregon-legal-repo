To Whom It May Concern:

This is a direct dispute under FCRA \S~1681s-2(a)(8) of Account *1001.

\subsection*{1. Active Balance Reported on a Debt Exeter No Longer Owns}
Exeter reports Account *1001 as a charge-off with an active balance of \textbf{\$19,095}. As shown by the attached correspondence from \textbf{Midland Credit Management dated December 30, 2024} (MCM Account 324372925), Midland now owns this debt and is collecting \textbf{\$16,103.48} on it. Once a debt is sold, the selling creditor must report a \$0 balance and the account as transferred/sold; reporting an active \$19,095 balance on a sold debt is a material inaccuracy under FCRA \S~1681e(b) and \S~1681s-2(a)(1)(A), and results in the same debt being double-reported by two entities.
