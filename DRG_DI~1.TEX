\section*{I.\quad The Controlling Fact: No Payment Was Ever Made, So the Date of First Delinquency Is Fixed}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item The Department's own system of record --- the National Student Loan Data System (NSLDS) --- reports a \textbf{Cumulative Payment Amount of \$0.00} and \textbf{no payment effective date} for each of these loans. \textbf{No payment has ever been made.}
  \item These loans entered repayment on \textbf{06/09/2019} (grace period end per NSLDS).
  \item The payment history the Department furnished to Equifax (report dated \textbf{09/24/2024}) shows these accounts \textbf{90 days past due (Nov.\ 2019), 120 days (Dec.\ 2019), 150 days (Jan.\ 2020), and 180 days (Feb.\ 2020).}
  \item Because \textbf{no payment was ever made, the accounts were never brought current.} Under FCRA \textbf{\S~1681c(c)(1)}, the date of first delinquency (``DOFD'') is the commencement of the delinquency that immediately preceded, and led to, the account's current derogatory status, and it \textbf{does not reset} unless the account is brought current. The DOFD is therefore \textbf{fixed no later than the first missed payment in 2019 (on or about July 1, 2019).} It cannot lawfully be moved forward.
\end{enumerate}

\section*{II.\quad Specific Inaccurate and Incomplete Information Furnished}

Each item below is disputed as inaccurate and/or incomplete in violation of FCRA \S~1681s-2(a)(1)(A) and the accuracy-and-integrity requirements of 12 C.F.R. \S\S~1022.42--1022.43.

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Re-aging of the Date of First Delinquency.} Current tradelines report a DOFD of \textbf{10/06/2024} (DRG collection lines) and \textbf{11/05/2024} (Nelnet lines). Both are false. They re-age a delinquency that began in 2019 and was never cured, unlawfully extending the seven-year reporting period under FCRA \S~1681c(a)(4)/(c)(1) from approximately \textbf{2026} to approximately \textbf{2031--2032}.
  \demand{Correct the DOFD to the true first-delinquency date (on or about \textbf{07/01/2019}) on every tradeline, or delete.}

  \item \textbf{Conflicting and false account status and balance.} The Nelnet tradelines report the accounts \textbf{closed/transferred with a \$0 balance} (consistent with ``paid/closed/zero balance''), while NSLDS reports the same loans as \textbf{``Defaulted, Unresolved''} with outstanding principal and interest. Simultaneously reporting a closed/zero-balance status and a defaulted outstanding balance is internally inconsistent and materially inaccurate.
  \demand{Reconcile to a single accurate status and balance, or delete.}

  \item \textbf{Delinquency reported during forbearance; forbearance/deferment history omitted.} NSLDS documents forbearance periods effective \textbf{08/08/2019, 02/06/2024, and 06/06/2024} (among others), as well as the COVID-19 administrative forbearance in effect from March 13, 2020. Furnishing 90-to-180-day-late marks during a documented forbearance window --- while omitting the forbearance/deferment status from the furnished payment history --- is both inaccurate and incomplete.
  \demand{Correct the payment history to reflect the actual forbearance and deferment periods and their effective dates, or delete.}

  \item \textbf{Duplicate reporting of the same debt.} The same underlying loans are being reported \textbf{twice} --- once as closed prior-servicer (Nelnet) tradelines, and again as separate DRG collection tradelines ``opened'' \textbf{11/07/2025} --- creating the false impression of multiple and/or newer obligations.
  \demand{Consolidate to a single accurate tradeline per loan, or delete the duplicates.}

  \item \textbf{Outdated furnisher/servicer identification.} Tradelines still identify \textbf{Nelnet} as servicer although NSLDS shows servicing transferred to the \textbf{Default Resolution Group effective 11/07/2025.}
  \demand{Correct the furnisher/servicer identification.}
\end{enumerate}

\section*{III.\quad Failure to Provide the Required 30-Day Pre-Default Notice and Opportunity for Review}

Borrower's Master Promissory Note, paragraph \textbf{20 (``Consumer reporting agency notification'')}, states verbatim:

\begin{quote}
``We will notify you at least 30 days in advance that we plan to report default information to a consumer reporting agency unless you resume making payments on the loan within 30 days of the date of the notice. You will be given a chance to ask for a review of the debt before we report it.''
\end{quote}

\noindent \textbf{Borrower received no such 30-day advance notice, and was given no opportunity to request a review of the debt before default information was furnished.} This obligation also implicates the pre-report notice requirements applicable to federal debts under the Debt Collection Act, \textbf{31 U.S.C. \S~3711(e)}, and the Department's Privacy Act procedures.

\demand{Produce \textbf{dated proof} that the required 30-day pre-report notice was sent to Borrower, together with proof of mailing/delivery and proof that Borrower was afforded the opportunity to request review. \textbf{Absent such proof, cease furnishing the default information and delete it from all CRAs.}}

\noindent \textbf{The Department's own furnished data forecloses any claim that the notice prerequisite was met.} On the Equifax report dated 03/01/2026, the Department furnished \textbf{Metro 2 Narrative Code 118 --- ``Customer Unable to Locate Consumer''} on each of these tradelines. That is the Department's own representation, and it cannot be reconciled with lawful furnishing of adverse information:

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{If Code 118 is accurate}, then the Department was unable to locate Borrower and therefore could not have provided the 30-day advance notice and opportunity for review that MPN \P~20 and 31 U.S.C. \S~3711(e) require before reporting --- meaning the delinquency and default information was furnished without satisfying that prerequisite.
  \item \textbf{If Code 118 is inaccurate} --- and NSLDS reflects a current address, cellular number, and email address for Borrower throughout the relevant period --- then the Department furnished \textbf{false information} about Borrower to the consumer reporting agencies.
\end{itemize}

\noindent Borrower does not concede that Borrower was unable to be located. Borrower holds the Department to its own certification. Under either reading, the delinquency and default information cannot stand as furnished.

\demand{Produce dated proof of the 30-day pre-report notice and review opportunity; and reconcile or delete Narrative Code 118. Absent proof of notice, delete the adverse information.}
