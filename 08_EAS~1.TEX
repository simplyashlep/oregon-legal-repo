\subsection*{1. Actively Disputed Debt Not Marked ``Disputed''}
The \$162 balance derives from Comcast/Xfinity charges that are the subject of an active, formal dispute (Comcast Case No.\ ESL05078608), in which Comcast has acknowledged billing errors. Under FDCPA \S~1692e(8), a collector may not report a disputed debt without communicating that it is disputed. Eastern Account System reports this account without a ``disputed by consumer'' notation, and the reported \$162 also exceeds the \$86 original amount without validation. This reporting fails FCRA \S~1681s-2(a)(1)(B) and \S~1681e(b).
