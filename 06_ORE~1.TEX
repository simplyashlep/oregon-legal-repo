To Whom It May Concern:

This is a direct dispute under FCRA \S~1681s-2(a)(8) and a validation demand under FDCPA \S~1692g regarding Account *1645.

\subsection*{1. Inaccurate, Inflated, Unvalidated Balance}
The account is reported at \textbf{\$20,508}, inflated from the \textbf{\$15,632} original amount. The Consumer demands validation of the debt and a full accounting of how the balance grew by \$4,876, including any post-judgment interest, fees, or costs added. Absent documented validation, the reported balance fails the FCRA maximum-possible-accuracy standard and must be corrected or deleted.

\subsection*{2. Possession Judgment / Doctrine of Merger}
The underlying matter was a landlord forcible-entry-and-detainer (eviction) proceeding by GMC Properties Corp, which obtained a judgment for possession. \textbf{To the extent a money judgment was also entered on this balance}, the pre-judgment contract debt merged into that judgment and can no longer be reported as a live consumer collection account; and under the National Consumer Assistance Plan standards adopted by all three nationwide bureaus, judgments are not reportable. The Consumer requests that Oregon Credit Systems state whether a money judgment was entered on this balance and, if so, cease reporting it as a collection account.

\subsection*{Demands}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*., leftmargin=*]
  \item Provide validation: the original account documentation, itemized balance, and the assignment establishing authority to collect and report.
  \item State whether a money judgment (not merely a possession judgment) was entered on this balance.
  \item Correct the inflated balance, or delete Account *1645 from all three bureaus.
  \item Respond in writing within 30 days.
\end{enumerate}
